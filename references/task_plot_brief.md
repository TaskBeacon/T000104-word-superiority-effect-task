Title: Reicher-Wheeler Word Superiority Task
Construct: contextual facilitation of letter identification

## Rows / representative conditions

- Word: four-letter English word, representative display `READ`; probe the second position with alternatives `E` and `O`.
- Pseudoword: pronounceable four-letter nonword, representative display `GARE`; probe the third position with alternatives `R` and `D`.
- Illegal string: unpronounceable four-letter string, representative display `XQRT`; probe the third position with alternatives `R` and `D`.
- Isolated letter: only `R` appears in the third of four spatial slots; probe the third position with alternatives `R` and `D`.

## Participant-visible timeline

- Word: fixation `+` (500 ms) -> `READ` (60 ms) -> jagged line-fragment mask (200 ms) -> position 2 marked, `E` left and `O` right (2500 ms max; F/J) -> fixation `+` (500 ms).
- Pseudoword: fixation `+` (500 ms) -> `GARE` (60 ms) -> jagged line-fragment mask (200 ms) -> position 3 marked, `R` left and `D` right (2500 ms max; F/J) -> fixation `+` (500 ms).
- Illegal string: fixation `+` (500 ms) -> `XQRT` (60 ms) -> jagged line-fragment mask (200 ms) -> position 3 marked, `R` left and `D` right (2500 ms max; F/J) -> fixation `+` (500 ms).
- Isolated letter: fixation `+` (500 ms) -> only `R` in slot 3 (60 ms) -> jagged line-fragment mask (200 ms) -> position 3 marked, `R` left and `D` right (2500 ms max; F/J) -> fixation `+` (500 ms).

## Accuracy notes

- All participant screens are white with black stimuli.
- The brief four-letter display is compact and centered; the isolated letter uses the same four-slot geometry.
- The mask is a dense rectangle of black irregular line fragments, not `####` and not a blank field.
- The post-mask probe, not the initial display, reveals the tested position.
- No correctness feedback appears.
- Conditions vary only in the brief display; the phase order and timing are identical.
